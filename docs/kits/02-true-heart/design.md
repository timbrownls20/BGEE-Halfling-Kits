# True Heart

*Halflings are not supposed to be paladins, and nobody told the True Heart. Raised on hearth-tales of good folk standing against the dark, they took up a short sword and the symbol of Arvoreen, the halfling god of defence and vigilance, and simply refused to be afraid. Their faith is quiet and stubborn: no charm can turn it, no terror can break it, and no demon has yet found the bottom of it.*

**Base class:** Fighter. **Race:** Halfling only. **Requires:** Charisma 15 or higher.

A halfling-only holy champion for Baldur's Gate 1 and 2, since halflings cannot be paladins in BG. It combines heavy armour and fighter weapon skill (capped at specialisation) with innate holy buffs and healing, a ladder of Heart immunities and an aura that steadies its allies. Target tier: a strong front-line healer and protector, rated 8.5 overall, just below the Fighter/Cleric: it has no spells through Shadows of Amn, but its Devas and Planetar give it the strongest late game of the halfling support kits.

## Advantages

- **AC bonus:** +2.
- **Protection from Evil:** once per day.
- **Armour of Faith:** 1 use at level 1, plus 1 more every 4 levels afterwards.
- **Draw Upon Holy Might:** 1 use at level 3, plus 1 more every 5 levels afterwards.
- **Righteous Magic:** 1 use at level 10, plus 1 more every 10 levels afterwards.
- **Lay on Hands:** 1 use at level 1, plus 1 more every 10 levels afterwards, healing 2 HP per level.
- **Cleansing Touch:** 1 use at level 5, plus 1 more every 5 levels afterwards. Touches one ally and removes harmful effects, growing stronger with level (see below).
- **Aura of Courage (passive, from level** 8): allies within 20 feet are immune to fear and gain +1 to saves, rising to +2 at level 16.
- **Heart immunities:** a new set of immunities at level 1, then at level 5 and every 5 levels after, from fear to maze and imprisonment at level 35 (see below).
- **Symbol of Arvoreen (level 25, automatic):** +2 Strength, +2 Constitution and regeneration of 2 HP per round.

## Cleansing Touch

Each tier adds to the ones before it. Cleansing Touch frees allies from effects at its own pace, often before or beyond what the True Heart is immune to itself.

| Level | Removes |
| --- | --- |
| 5 | Fear, poison and disease |
| 10 | Also hold, paralysis, sleep and slow |
| 15 | Also charm, confusion, stun and feeblemind |
| 20 | Also blindness, deafness and silence; restores drained levels |
| 25 | Also petrification and polymorph; heals the target for 25% of its maximum HP |

## Disadvantages

- Cannot use large shields. Any armour, including full plate, and medium shields are allowed.
- Cannot use two-handed swords, bastard swords, halberds, spears, longbows or heavy crossbows.
- Weapon skill capped at specialisation in short swords, maces, flails, short bows, slings and crossbows (light crossbows only, as an item restriction). Proficient only in all other weapons it can use.
- No spellcasting: all its holy powers are innate.
- Must be a lawful good halfling.
- **Falls if reputation drops to 8 or lower,** losing all kit abilities until reputation is restored.

## Balance notes

The True Heart has no spellcasting and no anti-mage tools: its holy power is innate. Its weapon skill is capped at specialisation, like BG2's other holy warriors, but full plate and a strong Aura of Courage make it a sturdy front-line protector. Armour of Faith, Draw Upon Holy Might and Righteous Magic mirror a fighter/cleric's best self-buffs without the XP split, while Lay on Hands and the healing chain (Mending Heart, Cleansing Heart, Undying Heart) provide healing, and Cleansing Touch rescues allies who are held, charmed, confused or, from level 25, petrified. Aura of Courage and the Heart ladder protect it and its allies from fear and mind-affecting effects. It has no magic resistance or elemental resistance of its own beyond strong saves (and Resist Magic if it takes it), and it relies on a party mage to strip enemy protections.

## Implementation notes

For anyone building this kit as a mod (for example with WeiDU):

- **Easy:** Heart immunities, AC bonus, Protection from Evil, innate Armour of Faith, Draw Upon Holy Might, Righteous Magic, Lay on Hands (as the paladin's) and Cleansing Touch (combining effects from existing cure spells), Symbol of Arvoreen, Heart of Faith, Healing Hands (extra uses of existing abilities), Mending Heart, Cleansing Heart and Undying Heart (based on standard mass healing and raising), Arvoreen's Messenger and Arvoreen's Champion (the standard Deva and Planetar summons, renamed).
- **Moderate:** Aura of Courage (a passive area effect around the True Heart) and Heart's Resolve (granting allies immunities by the True Heart's half level).
- **Hard:** the reputation-based fall condition (paladin falling is hard-coded and would need scripting for a fighter kit).

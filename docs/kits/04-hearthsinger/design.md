# Hearthsinger

*Far from home, halflings remember the songs of the hearth: the kettle on the fire, the harvest table, the faces of kin. The Hearthsinger, blessed by Cyrrollalee, the halfling goddess of hearth and hospitality, carries those songs into battle. One verse and tired companions stand straighter, strike harder and forget their fear; another and wounds close and, in time, the confused find their way back. Wherever the Hearthsinger stands, there is home, and home is worth defending.*

**Base class:** Cleric. **Race:** Halfling only.

A halfling-only cleric kit for Baldur's Gate 1 and 2: a priest who sings of hearth and kin. Each Hearthsong either invigorates allies with courage and strength or soothes them with rest and healing. It fights in the front line, but its real strength is what it does for the party. Replaces the earlier Holy Skald design.

## Advantages

- **Hearthsong:** 1 use per day at level 1, plus 1 more every 3 levels afterwards, up to a maximum of 8 uses per day at level 22 (see below).
- **Small and Nimble:** +2 AC from level 1.
- **Spellcasting:** the full cleric spell table, capped at 5th-level spells.
- Can achieve mastery in maces and slings.

## Disadvantages

- Cannot use large shields.
- Casting a spell while singing ends the current song early, and that Hearthsong use is spent: it cannot be restarted or resumed. Any remaining uses can still be sung later.
- Spells capped at 5th level (six higher-level spells come later as innate HLAs).
- Must be a halfling.

## Hearthsong

When used, the Hearthsinger chooses one mode. The song affects all allies within 30 feet and lasts 3 rounds, plus 1 round per level, up to level 20 (23 rounds maximum). The Hearthsinger can fight while singing. It can end the song early by casting a spell, but that use is spent and cannot be restarted, so breaking off to heal or raise an ally always costs the rest of the song.

Every HLA above is **New**: the Hearthsinger uses its own list instead of the standard cleric HLAs. The list allows 24 picks in total, a little more than one HLA per level from about 22 to 40. HLAs open at 3,000,000 XP, about level 22 for a cleric.

**Dropped standard cleric HLAs:** the whole standard cleric list, including Summon Deva (replaced by Hearth Guardians) and the cleric's offensive HLAs such as Aura of Flaming Death, Globe of Blades, Implosion and Storm of Vengeance.

## Balance notes

Personal power stays modest: casting a spell ends its song, and its weapon skill stops at mastery and costs scarce proficiency points. Party value is very high. A choice of songs, full cleric healing up to 5th level and party-saving HLAs put it second only to the Cleric/Mage as a supporter. Overall it sits among the stronger halfling kits, rated 8 overall; in Throne of Bhaal its innate Iron Skins and Shield of the Archons make it very hard to kill.

## Implementation notes

For anyone building this kit as a mod (for example with WeiDU):

- **Easy:** No One Left Behind (Raise Dead), mastery in maces and slings, the six innate spell HLAs (Heal, Iron Skins, Resurrection, Greater Restoration, Regeneration and Shield of the Archons as innate abilities, so the 5th-level spell cap doesn't get in the way).
- **Moderate:** the two Hearthsong modes, Song of Hearth and Home and Homeward Hymn (area auras as innate abilities), casting a spell ending the song (and spending that use) and Harmonic Song (casting no longer ends the song), Kin's Shield, Hearth Guardians (a custom ancestor-spirit creature, copied from a halfling warrior and the Summon Deva spell), and the cleric spell table capped at 5th level.
- **Hard:** none.

# Slinger

*Every halfling child learns the sling: for rabbits, for crows, for the occasional neighbour's window. The Slinger never stopped practising. A pouch of bullets and a leather cord are all they need to drop a charging orc at fifty paces, and by the time the giant works out where the stones are coming from, the small figure on the hill has already reloaded.*

**Base class:** Fighter. **Race:** Halfling only. **Requires:** Dexterity 16 or higher.

A halfling-only fighter kit for Baldur's Gate 1 and 2: a sling specialist built on the Archer's template, with Kensai-style scaling that never tails off. It hits like an Archer at range but is fragile if caught. Target tier: rated 8 overall, with damage (9) level with the Archer and Berserker.

## Advantages

- **Small and Nimble:** +2 AC and +2 movement from level 1.
- **Sling mastery:** can achieve grandmastery with slings, including grandmastery's extra attack per round, which in the Enhanced Editions applies to missile weapons as well as melee.
- **Scaling bonuses:** +1 to hit and +1 damage with slings at level 1, plus 1 more every 4 levels afterwards, with no cap (+5 from level 17, +10 from level 37).
- **Rapid Sling:** +½ attack per round with slings at levels 9, 18, 27 and 36 (+2 in total), reaching the 5 attacks per round cap at level 36 (2 base fighter attacks, +1 from grandmastery and +2 from Rapid Sling).
- **Called Shot:** 1 use at level 4, plus 1 more every 4 levels afterwards. The next sling hit within 1 turn applies extra effects by level (see below).

## Disadvantages

- Can only wear armour up to studded leather.
- Can only use bucklers and small shields.
- Cannot use daggers, axes, two-handed weapons, or any ranged weapon except slings.
- Limited to proficiency (one pip) in all other permitted weapons, like the Archer.
- Must be a halfling of good alignment.

**Crafted bullets:** the Slinger crafts three kinds of bullet (Dispelling, Breaching and True Shot), and only when the party rests in a city, town or inn, the same areas where the Magpie uses Brandobaris's Dice. Each kind can be crafted once every 24 hours, and each batch uses up one gem, ground into the bullets' cores. A standard batch uses a common gem: a Moonstone for Dispelling, a Bloodstone for Breaching and a Sunstone for True Shot. Once the Slinger has the matching Enhanced HLA, it can choose to craft an enhanced batch instead, from a costly gem: a Star Sapphire, a Diamond or an Emerald. With no gem of the right kind, no batch is made. Bullets keep, so the Slinger can stock up in town before a long expedition, but they only work in the Slinger's sling. Every batch grows by 1 bullet at level 30 and again at level 40, and Gem Cutter's Eye adds +2 to every batch per pick.

| Bullet | Standard batch | Enhanced batch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dispelling | 5 from a Moonstone | 8 from a Star Sapphire; dispels as if cast 5 levels higher |
| Breaching | 5 from a Bloodstone | 8 from a Diamond; also strips one spell protection per hit |
| True Shot | 10 +4 bullets from a Sunstone | 10 +5 bullets from an Emerald |

**Dropped standard fighter HLAs:** Whirlwind Attack and Greater Whirlwind Attack (removed), Deathblow, Greater Deathblow, War Cry, Power Attack and Smite (replaced by the Shots above), Hardiness (replaced by Out of Reach), and Resist Magic (replaced by Gem Cutter's Eye).

Dispelling and Breaching bullets make the Slinger the party's mage-breaker: a protected caster usually needs only one or two hits, so a batch of 5 covers two or three mage fights, or more with the 8-bullet Enhanced batches. Because bullets are only crafted in town, the Slinger stocks up before long dungeons and rations them through each one; the supply of Moonstones, Bloodstones and Sunstones limits how large that stock grows. Gems the Magpie finds with Brandobaris's Gift help keep it supplied. Enhanced batches need costly gems, so the Slinger saves its strongest bullets for the hardest fights and can fall back on standard batches when Star Sapphires and Diamonds run short. Enhanced Breaching adds spell-protection removal, so together Dispelling and Breaching bullets can take apart most layered ToB mages and liches. The Slinger chooses which fights get its special ammunition; the rest of the time it relies on its scaling sling damage and True Shot bullets.

## Balance notes

Rapid Sling brings its rate of fire close to a bow user's by level 18, and uncapped scaling means its per-bullet bonuses keep climbing through Throne of Bhaal. ToB offers +4 bullets and the Erinne Sling +5, which summons its own ammunition; the Slinger's own crafted True Shot (+4) and Enhanced True Shot (+5) bullets match or beat those. By the end of the game its sustained ranged damage likely edges past the Archer's. Unlike the Archer, nothing tails off: scaling is uncapped, Called Shot gains a new effect every 4 levels to 40, and Rapid Sling adds further steps at 27 and 36. It pays with studded leather, a small shield, proficiency-only melee and low survivability. Weak spots: missile-resistant skeletons (use melee backups) and mages behind Protection from Magical Weapons (handled by Breaching Shot bullets from its HLAs).

## Implementation notes

For anyone building this kit as a mod (for example with WeiDU):

- **Easy:** Small and Nimble, sling grandmastery and scaling bonuses, Sure Aim, Gem Cutter's Eye (more bullets per batch).
- **Moderate:** Rapid Sling (attack bonuses limited to slings), the extended Called Shot ladder (based on the Archer's Called Shot), Eagle Eye's bonuses and stun chance (on-hit effects for a set duration), Out of Reach (a repeating aura that knocks back enemies within 10 feet for 1 turn), and Dispelling, Breaching and True Shot with their Enhanced versions (custom bullets, the Dispelling and Breaching ones with on-hit effects, Breaching bullets set to enchantment 0 so Protection from Magical Weapons doesn't block them, made by a rest script that checks for an urban area and the 24-hour limit, and takes the matching gem).
- **Hard:** Eagle Eye's sling hits ignoring Mirror Image (an image absorbs the hit before any on-hit effect applies, so this probably needs EEex); Critical Strike ignoring critical-hit immunity (helmets block criticals in the engine, so this probably needs EEex).

### As built (not yet tested in game)

Code: `mod/halflingkits/kits/06-slinger/`. Where the build differs from the design, or relies on something to check in game:

- **Sling to-hit and damage** use the game's missile to-hit and missile damage bonuses. Slings are the kit's only missile weapon, so in practice they apply to slings only.
- **Rapid Sling** is a pulse every round that gives the extra attacks only while a sling is equipped: every sling in the game sets a spell state while equipped, and the pulse checks it. Check in game that the extra attacks appear with a sling and vanish with a melee weapon.
- **Called Shot** lasts 1 round and affects every sling hit in it, not just the next hit (TODO, EEex: the engine can't end an on-hit effect after one hit). Each hit refreshes the effects rather than stacking them. The knockback (from level 20) uses the Wing Buffet effect; check the push distance in game.
- **Eagle Eye**'s +2, stun chance and duration are as designed. Ignoring Mirror Image and Blur is not built (TODO, EEex).
- **Critical Strike** is the game's own, without its Power Attack prerequisite. Ignoring critical-hit immunity is not built (TODO, EEex).
- **Out of Reach** uses War Cry's enemy-only area (not exactly 10 feet). It is left out if War Cry isn't found.
- **Crafted bullets:** a block in the global scripts, one per party slot, crafts when the party rests in a city or in a building that isn't a dungeon. Once a day per kind, it takes one gem from the **Slinger's own inventory** and adds the batch to it. If the Slinger has the Enhanced HLA and carries the costly gem, it makes the enhanced batch; otherwise it makes the standard one. So the player chooses by what the Slinger carries: hand the Diamond to someone else to keep it. Gems are found by name at install; a missing gem means that batch can't be made. Check in game that a rest at an inn counts, that only one gem leaves a stack, and that a batch is made only once per rest.
- **Enhanced Dispelling** bullets always dispel, rather than dispelling as if 5 levels higher.
- **Bullets** are copies of the game's plain bullet, usable only by a Slinger. Breaching bullets are +0 and not magical.
- **Gem Cutter's Eye** counts its picks with four spell states, so each pick adds 2 bullets to every batch.
- **Weapon skill:** grandmastery in slings; at most proficiency in every other weapon it can use; none in daggers, axes, quarterstaffs, two-handed swords, halberds, spears, bows, crossbows and darts.

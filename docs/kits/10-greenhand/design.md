# Greenhand

*Sheela Peryroyl's priests keep no temples. They tend fields, hedgerows and orchards, mend whoever comes limping up the lane, talk to the badgers, and always know which way the wind is blowing. When something threatens the garden, the garden answers.*

**Base class:** Cleric. **Race:** Halfling only.

A halfling-only cleric kit for Baldur's Gate 1 and 2: a priest of Sheela Peryroyl, the halfling goddess of nature, harvests and weather, and the halflings' answer to the druid. It trades the cleric's holy and unholy magic for druid spells, so it heals, summons and scouts like a cleric while striking with nature's weapons. Because it uses the cleric XP table, it has no druid XP wall. Target tier: rated 8 overall, a strong healer and summoner with real area damage, but without the cleric's best control spells.

## Advantages

- **Weapon skill:** can reach grandmastery in slings, but only proficiency in clubs and quarterstaffs, and can put up to 2 pips in Sword and Shield Style. Clerics gain proficiency points slowly (2 at level 1, then one every 4 levels), so with every point in the sling it reaches grandmastery at level 12, and has both Sword and Shield pips as well by level 20. In the Enhanced Editions grandmastery's extra attack works with slings, giving the Greenhand a real ranged attack for rounds it isn't casting.
- **Small and Nimble:** +2 AC from level 1.
- **Green Thumb:** the Greenhand's summoned creatures gain +2 to hit, damage and saves, and last 50% longer. Its animals, insects, shambling mounds and treants are also immune to Death Spell: they're real creatures of the wild answering Sheela's call, not beings pulled from another plane. Elementals, the Aerial Servant and the Stalker are conjured as normal and can still be killed by it.
- **Sheela's Bounty:** its healing spells heal 25% more.
- **Wind's Whisper:** +2 to saves vs illusions. From level 5, it can cast Clairvoyance once per day as an innate ability, to scout ahead.
- **Harvest Loaf:** once per day (twice from level 12), the Greenhand shares out fresh bread from the harvest. Every ally within 20 feet heals 5 HP plus 1 per level and shakes off fatigue. From level 15 the loaf also gives +1 to saves for 1 turn. No halfling adventures on an empty stomach.
- **Find Familiar:** from level 1, the Greenhand can call a familiar once as an innate ability. It is always a rabbit, whatever the Greenhand's alignment, and it grows stronger as the Greenhand levels (table below). Like any familiar, it adds to its master's hit points and can be picked up and carried in the pack, where it heals. It isn't a summon, so it doesn't count towards the limit of 5, but Green Thumb's bonuses still apply. If it dies, the Greenhand loses the bonus hit points and 1 Constitution until it next rests, and can call the rabbit again after resting. That's softer than the usual familiar, whose death costs 1 Constitution for good.
- **Spell list:** the cleric spell list with the cuts below, plus druid spells, Icewind Dale spells (with the IWDification mod) and two spells of its own, Summon Horde and Sheela's Berries, all listed in the Spells section.

**The rabbit familiar by level.** It changes form automatically when the Greenhand reaches each level. It's a companion, not a fighter: it only nips (1d3) all game. What grows is how hard it is to kill, how much it heals, and what it can see: each form adds a divination, from spotting the invisible to True Seeing, so it stays worth keeping out through Throne of Bhaal. Each form keeps everything from the forms before it.

## Disadvantages

- Weapons limited to clubs, quarterstaffs and slings.
- Armour limited to studded leather or lighter (shields allowed).
- Cannot Turn Undead.
- Cannot cast the cleric's undead, spiritual weapon, mind-control, glyph, holy and unholy attack, poison and harm spells (full list below).
- Must be a halfling.

**New spell: Summon Horde (1st level).** The Greenhand calls the hedgerow's rabbits to its side: a horde of 6 to 8 rabbits pours out around it for 3 rounds plus 1 round per level (longer with Green Thumb). The rabbits don't fight, but they swarm about underfoot, and enemies often lose track of their real targets and attack the rabbits instead. A cheap, cheerful decoy that draws blows away from the party, especially early in the game. The rabbits are allies rather than summons, so they don't count towards the limit of 5 summoned creatures and don't crowd out the Greenhand's other summons.

**New spell: Sheela's Berries (1st level).** The Greenhand picks a handful of enchanted berries that keep for a day and can be eaten by anyone in the party. It makes 3 berries, plus 1 per 3 levels (up to 8). Each heals 1d4+1 HP, plus 1 per 4 levels, and as the Greenhand grows the berries cure more:

- Level 1: healing only.
- Level 5: also cure poison.
- Level 9: also cure disease.
- Level 14: also cure blindness, deafness and paralysis.

**Cut from the cleric list:**

- Undead: Animate Dead, False Dawn, and Turn Undead itself.
- Spiritual weapons: Spiritual Hammer, Flame Blade, Shillelagh, Magical Stone, and from IWDification Beast Claw, Moonblade and Star Metal Cudgel.
- Mind control: Command, Greater Command, Hold Person, Hold Animal, Rigid Thinking, Mental Domination, Confusion, Charm Person or Mammal (druid).
- Glyphs and symbols: Glyph of Warding, Symbol of Fear, Symbol of Stun, Symbol of Death.
- Holy and unholy attacks: Holy Smite, Unholy Blight, Flame Strike, Bolt of Glory, Holy Word, Unholy Word, Sunray, Sol's Searing Orb, Destruction.
- Holy warrior buffs: Draw Upon Holy Might, Holy Power.
- Poison and harm: Poison, Cause Serious Wounds, Slay Living, Harm, Dolorous Decay.
- Other: Blade Barrier, Gate, Doom, Find Traps, Chaotic Commands, Magic Resistance, Righteous Magic, Physical Mirror, Shield of the Archons, Know Alignment, and from IWDification Prayer, Favor of Ilmater, Produce Fire and Unfailing Endurance.

For control, the Greenhand relies on Entangle, Spike Growth, Spike Stones and Silence 15' Radius. Spell levels for the druid additions follow the druid list; check the exact level of each when implementing.

## Balance notes

The Greenhand gets the best of two lists: cleric healing, protection and divination, plus druid summons, Iron Skins and nature's area damage, on the cleric's XP table with no druid XP wall. Insect Plague and Creeping Doom are among the strongest spells in BG2, and Green Thumb makes its summons hit harder and last longer. In return it loses the cleric's best control (Hold Person, Command, Holy Word, the Symbols), its mind protection (Chaotic Commands), its melee and defensive buffs (Righteous Magic, Holy Power, Magic Resistance, Physical Mirror, Shield of the Archons), its holy attacks and Turn Undead, and fights in studded leather. Grandmastery in slings gives it a real ranged attack from the mid-game, but its proficiency points arrive slowly.

It overlaps with the Hearthsinger as the halfling party's priest: the Hearthsinger buffs with songs and wears plate, the Greenhand summons, scouts and strikes with nature's spells. A party would usually take one or the other.

**Rough ratings:** Damage 7, Survivability 6.5, Party value 8.5, Overall 8, Theme 10, Solo 8.

## Implementation notes

For anyone building this kit as a mod (for example with WeiDU):

- **Easy:** weapon and armour limits, Small and Nimble, removing Turn Undead, cutting cleric spells (the Enhanced Editions support per-kit spell exclusion), Clairvoyance as an innate ability, Harvest Loaf (an area heal), Sheela's Berries (a spell that creates healing items whose effects depend on caster level), Hedge Warden (the game's treant creature), Fair Winds and Sheela's Harvest (area effects).
- **Moderate:** Green Thumb (a script or effect that buffs the kit's summoned creatures and makes its natural ones immune to Death Spell), Sheela's Bounty and Bountiful Harvest (boosted healing on the kit's own cure spells), Summon Horde (the game's rabbit creature, created as allies so they don't count towards the summon limit), Find Familiar (a custom innate copy of the spell: the standard one is limited to the main character and picks the familiar by alignment, and the rabbit needs five creature files that swap as the Greenhand levels, like the Throne of Bhaal familiar upgrade), Garden of Friends and Call the Bog (summons using the game's existing animal and shambling mound creatures).
- **Hard:** adding druid spells to a cleric. Druid-only spells are flagged so clerics can't learn them, so the kit needs cleric-usable copies of those spells or EEex.

# Fleet Foot Fighter

*Barefoot and grinning, the Fleet Foot is gone before the blade falls. Raised on the hedge-lanes and orchard runs of halfling villages, these duellists never learned to stand still: they dart between legs, vault tables and leave only a blur where they stood. Bigger folk call it cheating. The Fleet Foot calls it a fair fight.*

**Base class:** Fighter. **Race:** Halfling only.

Oct 1, 2026 · @Tim

## Overview

A halfling-only fighter kit for Baldur's Gate 1 and 2: a barefoot, unarmoured duellist who wins through speed, extra attacks and time tricks rather than armour. Think of it as a halfling Kensai. It is rated 9 overall, the deadliest halfling kit, level with the Fighter/Cleric and above the Berserker.

## Advantages

- **AC bonus:** +2 at level 1, plus +1 every 5 levels afterwards.
- **Small Target:** +2 AC against missiles at level 1, rising to +4 at level 10.
- **Fleet of Foot:** +2 movement at level 1, plus +1 every 2 levels afterwards. Boots of Speed double the base movement rate, so the Fleet Foot draws level with a booted character at about level 15 and overtakes them from level 17, ending up the fastest character on the field.
- **Weapon speed:** -1 speed factor at level 1, plus 1 more every 5 levels afterwards.
- **Extra attacks:** +½ attack per round at level 1 and again at level 19.
- **Two-Weapon Style:** starts with 2 pips.
- **Grandmastery:** allowed in daggers, short swords, katanas and quarterstaffs.
- **Rapid Strike:** from level 7 (see below).
- **Razor's Edge:** every hit with a bladed weapon (dagger, short sword or katana) opens a wound that bleeds for 3 rounds. Bleeding deals 1 damage per round at level 1, rising by 1 every 10 levels (2 at 10, 3 at 20, 4 at 30, 5 at 40). Each hit adds its own wound, so bleeding stacks. Undead, golems, elementals and oozes do not bleed.

## Rapid Strike

Doubles the number of attacks per round, at a THAC0 penalty that disappears by level 19. 1 use per day at level 7, plus 1 more every 4 levels afterwards. Lasts 4 rounds at level 7, plus 1 round every 5 levels afterwards (5 at 12, 6 at 17, 7 at 22, and so on).

| Level | THAC0 penalty |
| --- | --- |
| 7 | -4 |
| 10 | -3 |
| 13 | -2 |
| 16 | -1 |
| 19+ | None |

## Disadvantages

- Cannot wear armour, helmets or boots, or use shields. Bracers, gauntlets, rings and cloaks are allowed.
- Weapons limited to daggers (including throwing daggers), short swords, katanas and quarterstaffs. The quarterstaff is its one blunt weapon and does not cause bleeding.
- No bows, crossbows, darts or slings, so throwing daggers are the only ranged option.
- Must be a halfling.

## Balance notes

The kit is a high-offence, medium-defence glass cannon rated 9 overall: the deadliest halfling kit, level with the Fighter/Cleric and above the Berserker. Passive Evasion is its main late-game defence.

| Kit | Offence | Defence | Utility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Berserker | High (rage) | Medium (rage immunities) | Low |
| Barbarian | High | Medium (late resistances) | Movement speed |
| Dwarven Defender | Medium | Very high | Low |
| Fleet Foot | High (APR, Rapid Strike) | Medium (scaling AC, Small Target, Evasion) | Very high movement, Time Stop HLAs |

Before Throne of Bhaal it has no protection against hold, slow or web and relies on a Ring of Free Action and halfling saves; the Relentless HLA closes that gap. Its time HLAs form a chain (Flicker Step, then Time Slow, then Time Pause), so its strongest burst combo, Rapid Strike with Time Pause, needs three HLA picks and arrives later in Throne of Bhaal. From level 19 it sits at the 5 APR cap, so ordinary Haste adds no attacks; Improved Haste and Rapid Strike still double them.

**Reading the numbers:** bleeding is shown separately, as the extra damage per round once a fight has run long enough for wounds to stack (about 3 rounds), and only against creatures that bleed. Against living enemies in a longer fight, late-game DPR reaches roughly 150 single-weapon and 185 dual-wielding, about level with a geared Kensai; against undead and golems it falls back to the weapon damage alone. During Rapid Strike it doubles its hits and its wounds, and in Throne of Bhaal Hamstring blunts whatever it is fighting rather than adding damage. Its THAC0 is overkill from mid-SoA. Defensively it relies on AC (-15 late with gear) and avoidance (Evasion, Afterimage, Flicker Step, speed); it has almost no physical resistance and only 20% magic resistance, so anything that gets past its AC hurts.

## Implementation notes

For anyone building this kit as a mod (for example with WeiDU):

- **Easy:** AC, movement and weapon speed bonuses, extra attacks, Time Slow and Time Pause (Time Stop effects), Relentless (standard immunities), Flicker Step (reuses the Shadowdancer's Shadowstep), Quicksilver (permanent magic resistance), Fleet Training (movement and weapon speed bonuses), Razor's Edge (damage over time on hit, like poison weapons, limited to bladed weapons and creatures that can bleed).
- **Moderate:** Rapid Strike (attack doubling with a THAC0 penalty that scales by level), Afterimage (Mirror Image flagged as undispellable), Evasion (half damage on failed saves needs a custom effect), Hamstring (needs a movement penalty that Free Action doesn't block).
- **Hard:** none.

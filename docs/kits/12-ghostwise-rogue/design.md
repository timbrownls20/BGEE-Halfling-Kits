# Ghostwise Rogue

*The Ghostwise keep to the deep Chondalwood, far from roads and towns. They speak mind to mind without a word, ride giant owls, and paint their faces before a hunt. The Ghostwise Rogue is what that life makes of a halfling: silent in the trees, savage in a fight, and quite unable to see the point of a locked door.*

**Base class:** Thief. **Race:** Halfling only. **Requires:** Constitution 14 or higher.

A halfling-only thief kit for Baldur's Gate 1 and 2: a wild hunter with a barbarian's rage and toughness, a thief's stealth and traps, and an owl familiar. It gives up the town thief's skills (no Open Locks or Pick Pockets), its magic items and its tricks with scrolls and potions. Target tier: rated 8 overall, the halfling ranger: a light fighter and scout rather than an assassin.

The placeholder called for a fighter/thief multiclass. The game doesn't support kits on multiclass characters without an extra mod, so it was rebuilt as a single-class thief with barbarian qualities (decided 16 October 2026): no dependency, and it works on every platform.

## Advantages

- **Rage:** 1 use per day at level 1, plus 1 more every 4 levels (5, 9, 13 and so on), as the Barbarian. For 5 rounds: +4 Strength, +4 Constitution, +2 to saves vs spells and -2 AC, and immune to charm, hold, fear, maze, stun, sleep, confusion and level drain. There is no fatigue when it ends.
- **Thick Hide:** resistance to slashing, crushing, piercing and missile damage, as the Barbarian: 10% at level 11, 15% at 15 and 20% at 19.
- **Hunter's Eye:** +1 to hit at level 1, plus 1 more every 4 levels, up to +6 at level 21. It closes half the gap between a thief's THAC0 and a fighter's, and applies to every attack.
- **Hunter's Pace:** +1/2 attack per round at level 13, and another 1/2 at level 21. A fighter gets these at 7 and 13.
- **Wild Hide:** +2 hit points per level to level 10, then +1 per level to level 20 (+30 in all), on top of the thief's d6. Early on that is close to a fighter's d10, so it can stand in a fight.
- **Fleet of Foot:** +2 movement, as the Barbarian.
- **Small and Nimble:** +2 AC.
- **Child of the Wood:** outdoors and away from towns, +1 damage and +15% to Hide in Shadows and Move Silently. Indoors, in towns and in dungeons it is an ordinary thief.
- **Weapon skill:** can reach specialisation (2 pips) in spears, axes, short swords, slings and short bows, and proficiency in its other weapons (clubs and daggers). Can put 3 pips (mastery) in Two-Weapon Style.
- **Thief skills:** Hide in Shadows, Move Silently, Find Traps, Set Traps and Detect Illusion, with 15 points per level to spread over five skills (as the Assassin) rather than the usual 25 over seven, so losing the town skills is a real cost. Set Traps only works outdoors (see Disadvantages).
- **Backstab:** as a thief, but the multiplier stops at x3: a hunter's ambush, not an assassin's.
- **Find Familiar:** from level 1, it can call an owl once per day as an innate ability (see below).

## Disadvantages

- **No town skills:** cannot Open Locks or Pick Pockets.
- **Snares belong in the wild:** can only set traps outdoors and away from towns. Indoors, in towns and in dungeons it can't set them at all, including Set Spike Trap.
- **Hunter's weapons only:** short swords, spears, clubs, axes, daggers, slings and short bows.
- **Armour:** leather and studded leather only: no Elven Chain or mithril, which other thieves rely on later. Bucklers only.
- **No Use Any Item, Scribe Scrolls or Alchemy:** the Ghostwise make nothing from paper or glass, and trust nothing they didn't make.
- Requires Constitution 14 or higher.
- Must be a neutral good, chaotic good or chaotic neutral halfling.

## The owl familiar

The Ghostwise bond with owls. The Rogue's owl is a scout that sees what hides and harries what runs, rather than a fighter. It grows as the Rogue levels. While it lives the Rogue gains bonus hit points. Each form keeps everything from the forms before it.

Like the Briarguard's badger and the Greenhand's rabbit, it is a simple summon (open question 9): it lasts one day, doesn't follow between areas or ride in the pack, and its death costs no Constitution.

## Balance notes

The Ghostwise Rogue is the halfling ranger: halflings can't be rangers in this game, and this kit fills that gap with a wilderness hunter, an animal companion and stealth, on a thief's chassis with a barbarian's rage. It is the toughest halfling thief and fights like a light fighter: Hunter's Eye closes half the THAC0 gap, Hunter's Pace gives it extra attacks at 13 and 21, and rage, Thick Hide, Wild Hide and its owl's bonus hit points let it stand in melee where other thieves hide. It pays with a d6 hit die, no Open Locks or Pick Pockets and only 15 skill points a level, a backstab that stops at x3, leather and studded leather with no Elven Chain or mithril, bucklers only, traps only outdoors, and no Use Any Item. Outdoors, Child of the Wood makes it a strong skirmisher; in cities and dungeons it is noticeably weaker. Its owl gives the party a second set of eyes (invisibility, then True Seeing), which matters for a party like The Wild Wood that otherwise relies on the Greenhand for divination.

Watch it against the Fleet Foot in melee: the Fleet Foot should stay the faster, harder-hitting duellist, which is why Wild Rage doubles the rage's length rather than adding an attack. Against the Briarguard (both nature warriors with a familiar and physical toughness), the Briarguard is the elemental tank with nature spells; the Ghostwise Rogue is the stealthy hunter with rage and has no elemental resistance.

**Rough ratings:** Damage 7, Survivability 7.5, Party value 7, Overall 8, Theme 9, Solo 7.5.

## Implementation notes

For anyone building this kit as a mod (for example with WeiDU):

- **Easy:** Rage (an innate copy of the Barbarian's rage, uses every 4 levels in the CLAB), Thick Hide (the Barbarian's own resistance steps), Hunter's Eye, Hunter's Pace (extra attacks in the CLAB), Wild Hide, Fleet of Foot, Small and Nimble, the backstab cap (x3 from the level where a thief would pass it), Tough as Roots, Silent Speech, Hunter's Mark, Vanish into the Wood, Call the Parliament, the armour and shield limits (base AC 7 or more, which also blocks Elven Chain and mithril; bucklers only).
- **Moderate:**
  - **Spears and axes:** a thief can't normally use them. They need opening to this kit only, as the Magpie's scrolls and wands were: clear the "unusable by thief" flag, then block every other thief kit.
  - **No Open Locks or Pick Pockets:** lower both skills out of reach, as the Magpie's Set Traps. **15 skill points per level:** a row in `THIEFSKL.2DA`, as the Magpie's.
  - **Child of the Wood and outdoor-only traps:** a global-script check per party slot that applies the outdoor bonus when the area is outdoors and not a city, and removes it otherwise (like the True Heart's fall check). The same check takes Set Traps away indoors (the skill lowered out of reach while inside) and sets a spell state outdoors; the kit's copy of Set Spike Trap only works with that state.
  - **The owl familiar:** the badger's and rabbit's approach (`HK_FAMILIAR`): a familiar for the main character, a one-day summon for anyone else. It needs an owl or bird creature to copy; if the game has none, another small creature stands in.
  - **Wild Rage and Spirit of the Chondalwood:** spell states that the rage checks, as the Surefoot's stances.
- **Hard:** none.

# Ghostwise Rogue

*Placeholder: not designed yet.*

**Base class:** Fighter/Thief (multiclass). **Race:** Halfling only. **Requires:** to be decided.

A twelfth halfling kit: a Ghostwise halfling rogue. The Ghostwise are the reclusive, wild halflings of the Chondalwood. This one fights like a barbarian and moves like a hunter, but has no use for town tricks.

## Starting ideas

- **Base:** fighter/thief multiclass.
- **Barbarian qualities:** for example a rage, extra hit points or toughness, and fast movement. To be chosen.
- **No town skills:** cannot Open Locks or Pick Pockets.
- **Wilderness bonuses:** stronger outdoors and in the wild. Details to come.

# Magpie

*Every halfling village has one: the cousin who comes home from the woods with a silver spoon, a lost ring and somebody's spare boots, and swears they just found them. Brandobaris, the halfling god of thieves, luck and adventure, smiles on the Magpie. Treasure has a way of turning up wherever it sleeps, and the dice rarely roll against it.*

**Base class:** Thief. **Race:** Halfling only. **Requires:** Charisma 15 or higher, Intelligence 9 or higher.

A halfling-only thief kit for Baldur's Gate 1 and 2 built on luck rather than murder. It trades the backstab for a knack with wands and scrolls, and keeps the party supplied with treasure it finds while resting and gold it wins at the dice tables. Target tier: among the stronger single-class thieves, with unusual party value.

## Advantages

- **Luck:** +1 at level 1, plus 1 more at levels 10 and 20 (+3 in total).
- **Ducking and Weaving (AC bonus):** +2 AC for every point of Luck the Magpie has, granted alongside each Luck increase. Small, quick, hard to pin down, and favoured by Brandobaris.
- **Single Weapon Style:** can put 2 proficiency points into Single Weapon Style, gaining its AC and critical-hit bonuses when fighting with one weapon and no shield. The off-hand stays free for wands and scrolls.
- **Magpie's Eye:** +15% to Pick Pockets, +10% to Open Locks and Move Silently, +20% to Detect Illusion, and +10 Lore at level 1, plus +2 Lore every level afterwards. A Magpie slips in, pockets the shiny thing and slips out, and can tell a priceless trinket from a pretty fake, or a real mage from a mirror image, at a glance.
- **Magpie's Knack:** learns to use magic meant for others, one piece at a time: scrolls, wands, then other classes' items (see below).
- **Silver Tongue:** 1 use per day at level 1, plus 1 more at levels 6, 11, 16 and every 5 levels after. When a pickpocket goes wrong, or someone turns hostile, the Magpie talks its way out. It works only on non-plot characters no higher in level than the Magpie: the target must save vs spell, at -1 for every point of the Magpie's Charisma above 15, or calm down and stop being hostile, as if nothing happened.
- **Brandobaris's Gift (on rest):** once per day (24 hours of game time), when the party rests, there is a 50% chance the Magpie finds an item, rolled on BG2's own random treasure tables. The tier used rises with the Magpie's level: BG1-level treasure at levels 1 to 8, early SoA at 9 to 16, late SoA at 17 to 24, and Throne of Bhaal from 25. Weapons and armour are excluded from the rolls, so finds are potions, scrolls, gems, ammunition and gold, plus the occasional low-charge wand from the late SoA tier onwards, and never break item progression.
- **Brandobaris's Dice (urban rest):** when the party rests in a city, town or inn, the Magpie gambles through the night. Party gold changes by up to about 50 gold × its level either way, with odds in its favour (about 60/40), improved by its Luck. Once per day.

**Fumble chance:** magic doesn't come naturally to a thief, so its scrolls and wands can fail: 25% at level 1, dropping by 5% every 4 levels to 0% at level 21.

## Disadvantages

- **No backstab,** and cannot set traps (no Set Traps skill and no trap HLAs). It can still find and disarm traps.
- Weapons limited to daggers, short swords, clubs, quarterstaffs, slings, darts and short bows.
- Gains only 20 thief skill points per level, instead of the usual 25.
- Armour limited to leather and studded leather; no shields.
- Must be a neutral good, chaotic good or chaotic neutral halfling.

## Balance notes

In combat the Magpie is a modest thief, with no backstab and limited weapons. Its combat bite comes from wands and scrolls rather than weapons: strong bursts, but finite charges and a fumble chance early on, so it must manage its hoard. Its value is economic and logistical: a steady stream of potions, scrolls, gems, ammunition and gold over a whole run. Brandobaris's Gift and Brandobaris's Dice are each limited to once per day, so neither can be farmed by resting repeatedly; even with Brandobaris's Favour and both Treasure Hunter picks, the Magpie finds at most three items a day, plus the scroll copies from Forger's Hand. Silver Tongue only works on non-plot characters no higher in level than the Magpie, so it cannot talk its way past quest fights or bosses. It gains only 20 thief skill points per level instead of 25, so it builds its skills more slowly than most thieves and must choose where to spend them. Single Weapon Style and Magpie's Eye's stealth and lock bonuses keep it a capable rogue between charges, and Master Tinker keeps its favourite wands going through long dungeons. Overall it sits among the stronger single-class thieves through loot, magic items and crowd control, though not on raw damage or skills.

## Implementation notes

For anyone building this kit as a mod (for example with WeiDU):

- **Easy:** Charisma requirement, Magpie's Eye, Luck and AC bonuses, Slip Away, Brandobaris's Grin, Trickster's Turn, Misdirection (an area pacify effect), Smooth Talker, Pocket Sand (a blinding effect), removing trap-setting, weapon and armour limits, removing backstab.
- **Moderate:** Silver Tongue (a charm-style effect that also returns a hostile target to neutral), Master Tinker (extra wand recharges), Forger's Hand (part of the same rest script as Brandobaris's Gift: it checks every party member's inventory, picks a random scroll within the level cap and gives the Magpie a copy; the largest single script in the doc), Brandobaris's Gift, Brandobaris's Favour and Treasure Hunter (scripts that run on resting and draw from the random treasure tables), Brandobaris's Dice (a rest script that also checks the area type), and Magpie's Knack (scroll, wand and item use unlocked by level, with a fumble chance early on, needs per-level checks).
- **Hard:** none.

### As built (not yet tested in game)

Code: `mod/halflingkits/kits/07-magpie/`. Where the build differs from the design, or relies on something to check in game:

- **Magpie's Knack (open question 8, decided 16 October 2026):** split by category. The base component lets the Magpie use arcane scrolls and wands from level 1. Mage, bard and cleric items come with Use Any Item. The level ladder and the fumble chance are an EEex TODO: the game's per-kit item rule can check a kit but not a level, and a scroll can't be made to fail partway. At install, every scroll and wand that a mage can use but a thief can't is opened to single-class thieves, then blocked again for the kitless thief and every other thief kit. Multi-class thieves are unaffected. Thief kits added by mods installed after this one are not blocked; check the order in game.
- **No backstab:** the multiplier is set to x1. **No trap setting:** the Set Traps skill is lowered out of reach; check in game that the button does nothing or that points put into it are wasted.
- **20 thief skill points per level:** set in the game's thief skill point table (`THIEFSKL.2DA`, where the Assassin's 15 comes from). The install copies the kitless thief's row for the Magpie with 20 in the per-level column. If the table, that row or that column isn't found, the install warns and the Magpie keeps the usual 25. Check the points on level-up in game.
- **Silver Tongue:** the target saves vs spell at a fixed -2, because a spell can't read the Magpie's Charisma. Targets above the Magpie's level are unaffected, using a SPLPROT row the mod adds ("level greater than"); check in game. It turns the target neutral. It can't tell plot characters apart, so use it with care on quest characters.
- **Brandobaris's Gift:** a block in the global scripts, one per party slot, runs once a day on any rest. The treasure tiers are built at install from the game's own items: potions, scrolls, gems and ammunition by shop price, plus wands (with 2 charges) from level 17. Only movable, uncursed, non-quest items count; weapons and armour never do. Each tier holds up to 50 items. **Brandobaris's Favour** always finds something and draws from the next tier up; on the top tier it just always succeeds, rather than rolling twice. **Treasure Hunter** adds a roll per pick.
- **Brandobaris's Dice:** once a day, on a rest in a city or a building that isn't a dungeon, the Magpie wins (60%) or loses (40%) a set amount by level band: 100 gold at levels 1 to 4, rising to 1,500 from level 30. Luck doesn't change the odds.
- **Tinker's Touch, Master Tinker and Forger's Hand:** not built (TODO, EEex). No opcode restores wand charges, and a script can't list the party's scrolls. They are left out of the HLA list.
- **Misdirection** is a sleep that ends when the target is hit, for 2 rounds, in War Cry's enemy-only area, with a save vs spell at -2.
- **Slip Away** applies the game's Improved Invisibility and Non-Detection.
- **Pocket Sand:** each pick gives 3 uses a day.
- **Weapon skill:** the thief's, in the weapons the Magpie may use, with 2 pips in Single Weapon Style.

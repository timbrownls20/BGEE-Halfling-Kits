# Surefoot Fighter

*Some halflings run from trouble. The Surefoot plants its bare heels and lets trouble break against it. Raised on hard roads and long winters, they walk all day without tiring and cannot be moved by fear, magic or a charging ogre. When the line wavers, look for the small figure that hasn't taken a step back.*

**Base class:** Fighter. **Race:** Halfling only. **Requires:** Constitution 15 or higher.

A halfling-only defensive fighter kit for Baldur's Gate 1 and 2. It is built to be hard to kill rather than to deal damage: it trades the Dwarven Defender's heavy armour and slow stance for extra hit points, steadily rising saves and a long ladder of immunities and resistances. Target tier: rated 8 overall, the toughest kit in the doc, a step above the Dwarven Defender.

## Advantages

- **AC bonus:** +2 at level 1.
- **Stand Fast:** 1 use at level 1, plus 1 more at levels 5, 10, 15 and every 5 levels after.
- **Halfling Stride:** every 2 levels, starting at level 2 (see below).
- **Cold resistance:** +20% (cosy toes).
- **Wayfarer's Pace:** +1 movement at level 1, plus +1 every 2 levels afterwards. It draws level with a character in Boots of Speed at about level 17 and edges ahead from level 19, always a step behind the Fleet Foot. The Surefoot walks all day without tiring, so it never slows the party for long.
- **Hold the Line (from level 6):** 1 use, plus 1 more at levels 12, 18, 24, 30 and 36. The Surefoot roots itself and shields allies around it (see below).

## Disadvantages

- Can only wear armour up to splint mail.
- Can only use bucklers and small shields.
- Grandmastery in short swords, flails and maces. Mastery in slings, short bows, clubs and daggers. Cannot use any other weapon.
- Cannot wear boots.
- The kit's own abilities never take physical or elemental (fire, cold, electricity and acid) damage resistance above 90%; gear can still add more, so watch for totals above 100%, which heal instead of harm.
- Must be a lawful good, neutral good or lawful neutral halfling.

## Stand Fast

Activated ability, lasts 1 turn. It starts weak and grows into a late-game powerhouse:

- **Physical resistance:** 15% at level 1, plus 5% more at levels 5, 10, 15, 20 and 25, reaching 40% at level 25.
- **Constitution:** +1 at level 1, +2 at level 10, +3 at level 20. Temporary Constitution adds hit points, so each use makes the Surefoot harder to kill.

## Hold the Line

Activated ability. The Surefoot cannot move for 1 turn: it is rooted in place, which is the trade-off. All allies within 15 feet, including the Surefoot, gain the effects below. It stacks with Stand Fast.

## Balance notes

The Surefoot is the hard-to-kill halfling: modest damage, but extra hit points from Constitution, steady physical resistance and the broadest immunity ladder of any warrior kit. Scaled Stand Fast makes it a late bloomer: fragile for the first few levels, average by level 10, and among the sturdiest warriors in the game by Throne of Bhaal. Charm and sleep immunity at 12 protects it through SoA's mind flayers and vampires; Weathered and Storm Strides at 18 and 20 arrive with ToB's dragons and elemental casters. Stand Fast and the strides take its physical resistance to 90% against every physical damage type by level 40, and its own abilities go no higher, so it is never fully immune to weapons from the kit alone. Still Ground, its one answer to enemy casters, shuts down all spellcasting nearby, its own party's included. Watch gear: past 100%, BG2 heals a character instead of harming it.

## Implementation notes

For anyone building this kit as a mod (for example with WeiDU):

- **Easy:** stride immunities, saves and resistances, Stand Fast and its Braced, Warded and Unbreakable Stance upgrades, Constitution bonuses, Wayfarer's Pace, Stubborn, Deathless Stride (Death Ward), Second Wind (healing), Shrug It Off (a touch that cures effects on an ally).
- **Moderate:** Hold the Line and Wider Line (an area buff that also roots the user), Still Ground (an area that disables spellcasting, like Silence without a save), Last Stand (a Contingency-style trigger at below 25% HP).
- **Hard:** Unmoving Stride's Time Stop immunity (Time Stop is a global freeze, so this needs testing and probably EEex).

### As built (not yet tested in game)

Code: `mod/halflingkits/kits/03-surefoot-fighter/`. Where the build differs from the design, or relies on something to check in game:

- **Unmoving Stride's Time Stop immunity** is not built (TODO, EEex): Time Stop freezes everyone but its caster through the engine, with no effect to block. The level-40 stride still gives its 2% physical resistance and +1 to saves.
- **The 90% cap:** Hold the Line's physical resistance goes to the Surefoot's allies only, not to the Surefoot. Its own physical resistance is then the strides (50% at level 40) plus Stand Fast (40%), which is exactly 90%. Hold the Line still gives the Surefoot its AC, saves and immunities.
- **Tireless Stride's fatigue immunity** is a -100 fatigue modifier, because fatigue is the engine's rest timer, not an effect that can be blocked. Check in game that a tired Surefoot shows no fatigue penalty.
- **Hold the Line** uses Bless's party-only area (not exactly 15 feet). The Surefoot's movement is set to 0 for the turn. **Wider Line** doesn't widen the area; it gives the extra use and +10% physical resistance to allies.
- **Stand Fast's stance upgrades** are spell states set by the HLAs; Stand Fast applies the one version that matches them. Check in game that exactly one version lands.
- **Unbreakable Stance** regenerates 3 HP per round, as designed. **Last Stand** regenerates 6 HP per round, not 5: the game's regeneration steps can't give 5. It recovers one game day after it fires, not on rest.
- **Still Ground** uses the area of Silence 15' Radius around the Surefoot (15 feet, not 20). Every round for 3 rounds it stops wizard, priest and innate casting for 1 round for everyone in the area. Check in game that the area centres on the Surefoot and moves with it.
- **Death magic** (Deathless Stride and Last Stand) blocks instant death, slay effects and Power Word: Kill.
- **Resist Magic** and **Smite** are the game's own HLAs; Smite loses its Power Attack prerequisite.
- **Weapon skill:** grandmastery in short swords, flails and maces; mastery (3 pips) in slings, short bows, clubs and daggers.

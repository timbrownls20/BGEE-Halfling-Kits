# Unbeguiled

*Wizards are used to people being afraid of them. The Unbeguiled halfling just isn't. Charms slide off, illusions look faintly silly, and the grandest spell in the archmage's book seems somehow not to take. Small, quiet and entirely unimpressed, it walks up to the caster that everyone else is running from, and pokes it with something sharp.*

**Base class:** Fighter. **Race:** Halfling only.

A halfling-only fighter kit for Baldur's Gate 1 and 2: a halfling Wizard Slayer, built around shrugging off magic and spoiling other people's magic. Where the Magpie collects and uses magic, the Unbeguiled distrusts it, resists it and breaks it. Unlike the Wizard Slayer, it keeps most magic items, so it is a genuine mage-hunter rather than a curiosity. Target tier: a solid fighter kit with a unique role as the party's mage-hunter and its shield against magic.

## Advantages

- **Stubborn Will:** 10% magic resistance at level 1, plus 1.5% every level afterwards (40% at level 21, 68% at level 40). +2 to saves vs spells and wands. Immune to charm from level 1, to confusion from level 6, and to domination from level 10: it simply can't be beguiled.
- **Clear Heads:** an aura that shares the Unbeguiled's stubbornness with allies within 20 feet, a step behind its own: immunity to charm from level 8, to confusion from level 16, and to domination from level 24. Nobody standing next to a halfling this unimpressed can be talked into anything.
- **Snap Out Of It:** a touch that frees one ally from magical meddling. It covers what Clear Heads doesn't, and grows with level: once per day from level 3, ending sleep and charm; twice per day from level 9, adding silence; three times per day from level 15, adding feeblemind. From level 9 it also ends confusion and domination, for allies the aura doesn't yet protect.
- **Sees Through It:** +2 to saves vs illusion spells. From level 9, it can use True Sight as an innate ability once per day, plus once more at levels 15 and 21. From level 12, its attacks ignore Mirror Image and Blur: a mirror image is just a trick of the light.
- **Small and Nimble:** +2 AC from level 1. Big folk, and the things big folk summon, keep swinging over its head.
- **Spoiling Strike:** the Unbeguiled's answer to the Archer's Called Shot, aimed at casters instead. Once activated, every hit in the next round, melee or ranged, carries the effects below. For that round its weapons count as non-magical, so Protection from Magical Weapons can't stop the hits or the Breach they carry (but creatures that need magical weapons to hit, and anyone under Protection from Normal Weapons, can't be hurt that round). Effects add up as it levels. Usable once per day at level 1, plus once more every 5 levels (8 uses at level 36).
  - Level 1: each hit adds a 15% chance to fail spells, arcane and divine alike, for 3 rounds, stacking up to 60%.
  - Level 6: the target also takes -2 to saves vs spells for 3 rounds.
  - Level 12: the first hit Breaches the target, stripping its combat protections such as Stoneskin, Protection from Magic Weapons and Mantle. No save, and magic resistance doesn't stop it.
  - Level 18: each hit also lowers the target's magic resistance by 10% for 3 rounds, stacking up to 30%, opening it up to the party's own casters.
- **Make-Believe:** from level 12, as an innate ability once per day, the Unbeguiled declares that the summoned creatures around it simply aren't real, and they're destroyed, as Death Spell destroys summons. It affects summoned creatures only. Once more at levels 18 and 24.
- **Weapon skill:** grandmastery in daggers, short swords, slings and short bows; mastery in clubs and darts.

## Disadvantages

- Weapons limited to small weapons: daggers, short swords, clubs, darts, slings and short bows.
- Can't use large shields.
- Will not wear magical rings, amulets or cloaks, and won't use wands or scrolls, not even protection scrolls. It simply does not trust them.
- Lore is 0 at every level and can't be raised: it has no interest in what magic items do, so it can't identify them.
- Must be a halfling.

**Dropped standard fighter HLAs:** Whirlwind Attack, Greater Whirlwind Attack, Greater Deathblow, Deathblow, Smite, Power Attack and War Cry (replaced by the Unbeguiled abilities above), and Resist Magic, which would lower its own magic resistance rather than raise it.

## Balance notes

The Unbeguiled is a specialist hunter of spellcasters. Magic resistance and strong saves let it walk up to a mage through its spells; ignoring Mirror Image and Blur means it actually lands its hits; Spoiling Strike breaks the mage's combat protections, makes its spells fail and lowers its magic resistance for the party's casters; Disenchanting Strike strips its spell protections. Against non-casters it is a sturdy but ordinary fighter with small weapons. It is much stronger than the Wizard Slayer, which can't use most magic items beyond weapons and armour, while the Unbeguiled gives up only magical rings, amulets and cloaks, wands, scrolls and large shields, and has grandmastery in its main weapons, including slings and short bows for shooting casters from range. It is also the party's shield against magic: Clear Heads keeps allies from being charmed, confused or dominated, Hearth Wisdom shares its magic resistance, Unshakeable Wards stops enemy mages stripping the party's buffs, Steady Company stops them silencing, slowing or holding allies, and Stand Behind Me pulls hostile spells onto itself. Its own magic resistance stops at about 68%, so it is never fully immune. Clear Heads doesn't overlap with the True Heart's Aura of Courage, which covers saves and fear.

## Implementation notes

For anyone building this kit as a mod (for example with WeiDU):

- **Easy:** magic resistance and save bonuses, weapon and item limits, True Sight (the True Seeing spell as an innate ability), Make-Believe (a summons-only version of Death Spell as an innate ability), Clear Heads (an immunity aura), Snap Out Of It (a touch that removes magical effects), Steady Company (an immunity aura).
- **Moderate:** Spoiling Strike (an activated ability built like the Archer's Called Shot, with spell failure based on the Wizard Slayer's ability but applied to both arcane and divine spells, plus Breach and lowered magic resistance on hit), Disenchanting Strike (a Secret Word effect on hit), Hearth Wisdom (a magic resistance aura), Unshakeable Wards (immunity to the dispel and Breach effects for allies in range).
- **Hard:** Stand Behind Me (redirecting spells aimed at allies to the Unbeguiled; probably needs EEex), ignoring Mirror Image and Blur (an image absorbs the attack before any on-hit effect runs, so this probably needs EEex), Critical Strike ignoring critical-hit immunity (helmets block criticals in the engine, so this probably needs EEex).
- **Check:** friendly healing and buff spells are flagged to bypass magic resistance, so they still land despite its magic resistance. Spot-check any modded or unusual party buffs for the same flag. Make Spoiling Strike's Breach bypass magic resistance the same way.
- **Check:** on-hit effects only apply if the hit lands. Spoiling Strike's weapons count as non-magical for its round (enchantment 0), so Protection from Magical Weapons doesn't block them. Test whether Stoneskin absorbs the hit before the Breach applies; if it does, the Breach may need to fire on the attack itself, which probably needs EEex.

# Strength Rankings

Three separate rankings: Strength (offensive power), Survivability and Party value. Each class appears in all three, so the trade-offs are visible: the Kensai sits near the top of Strength and near the bottom of Survivability, while the Surefoot does the reverse. Rankings assume unmodded BG2:EE on normal to hard difficulty and are approximate.

All three rankings cover a full BG1 to Throne of Bhaal run, leaning late. Full casters lead Strength because high-level spells decide the hardest fights, though they are much weaker early. Multiclasses trail single classes early because of the XP split, and multiclass warriors are capped at specialisation. In BG2, the only multiclass open to halflings is Fighter/Thief, which is part of what makes the halfling kits appealing.

## Notes on the new kits

- **Fleet Foot:** top melee damage and the best mobility in the game. Gauntlets and bracers, which the Kensai cannot use, put it ahead of the Kensai in practice, and Flicker Step lets it reach kiting casters.
- **Slinger:** sustained ranged damage that never tails off, reaching 5 sling attacks per round at level 36. Fragile up close; Eagle Eye adds control, and Breaching and Dispelling Shot strip enemy mages' protections.
- **True Heart:** the most generous kit, a heavily armoured, specialisation-capped fighter with innate holy buffs and healing (Lay on Hands, Cleansing Touch, Mending Heart), Aura of Courage and the Heart immunity ladder. If playtesting feels too easy, move charm and sleep immunity from level 10 to 15.
- **Tymora's Champion:** a lucky all-rounder. Lucky Dodge and Lucky Break turn blows aside, and Second Chance saves the whole party once a day.
- **Surefoot:** the toughest kit in the doc, with near-complete immunities and physical resistance capped at 90%. Hold the Line and Still Ground anchor the party (Still Ground stops the party's own casting too); its damage is the lowest of the fighters.
- **Hearthsinger:** modest personally, but one of the best supporters in the game: two songs, cleric healing to 5th level (6th and 7th through HLAs), Homeward Hymn and Kin's Shield.
- **Magpie:** a thief built on luck and loot rather than murder: no backstab and no traps, but Magpie's Knack lets it use scrolls, wands and other classes' items, while Brandobaris's Gift and Dice keep the party supplied.
- **Unbeguiled:** the halfling Wizard Slayer done properly. Magic resistance to about 68%, immunity to mind control that it shares with allies through Clear Heads, and Spoiling Strike to Breach and spoil any caster. Its HLAs make it the party's shield against magic. Ordinary against enemies that don't cast, and its AC suffers without magic cloaks, rings or amulets.
- **Nettle:** a thief with no backstab multiplier; its backstabs and traps carry an anti-magic package instead. Its traps get through Protection from Magical Weapons and Stoneskin, which makes it the best kit in the doc at taking apart a protected caster, but its own damage is low.
- **Greenhand:** the halflings' druid: cleric healing and divination, druid summons and nature's area spells (Insect Plague, Creeping Doom) on the cleric XP table, in studded leather.
- **Briarguard:** the halfling nature warrior. No metal armour, but Barkskin, Iron Skins and elemental resistance up to 80% and immunity to hold and slow make it a hard front-liner, with Entangle, a plant guardian and a badger familiar on the side. Grandmastery in every weapon and Strength of the Oak give it solid damage.

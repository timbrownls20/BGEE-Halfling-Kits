All eleven halfling kits sit between 7.5 and 9, with the Fleet Foot as the deadliest at 9, level with the Fighter/Cleric; the True Heart follows at 8.5 and the rest sit just below it, well below the full casters, and well above the weakest kits. The Beastmaster rates lowest of all: it cannot use metal weapons and its animal summons fall off badly after BG1. The Wizard Slayer is close behind, trading most of its power for spell disruption that rarely decides fights.

## Playtest checklist

Each kit is playtested once, in one of three themed parties of four with one rogue each (The Bold, Hearth and Home, The Wild Wood); the line-ups are under Playtest parties in Party Design, and build progress in Tasks.

The main things to watch in play, with the first trim to try for each if it feels too strong.

| Kit | Watch for | First trim |
| --- | --- | --- |
| True Heart | Charm and sleep immunity at level 10 making SoA's mind flayer and vampire fights too easy | Move charm and sleep immunity to level 15 |
| Fleet Foot | Rapid Strike combined with Time Pause and Time Slow killing bosses before they act (gated behind the Flicker Step, Time Slow, Time Pause chain) | Limit Time Slow to 1 pick |
| Tymora's Champion | Second Chance making the hardest fight of the day feel safe | Cut its radius to 20 feet or its heal to 30% |
| Slinger | Improved Haste at level 36 (about 10 sling attacks per round), and Eagle Eye stun-locking single targets | Limit Eagle Eye to 2 picks or 5 rounds |
| Slinger | Breaching Shot and Dispelling Shot making enemy mages too easy | Cut each Breaching batch from 5 bullets to 3, or switch it to a rarer gem |
| Hearthsinger | Homeward Hymn's party-wide extra attack, and Kin's Shield covering every hard fight | Remove the Hymn's extra attack; limit Kin's Shield to 1 pick |
| Surefoot | Near-invulnerability late in Throne of Bhaal | Lower Stand Fast's maximum from 40% to 30% |
| Surefoot and Hearthsinger | Feeling too weak early in BG1, before their abilities scale | Give the Surefoot +1 to hit at level 1; give the Hearthsinger an extra Hearthsong use at level 1 |
| Magpie | Scrolls and wands from Magpie's Knack winning BG1 fights despite the fumble chance | Raise the starting fumble chance to 35% |
| Unbeguiled | Whether Stand Behind Me (+25% magic resistance, to 95%) really soaks redirected spells, and whether Spoiling Strike's Breach lands often enough, since it needs the hit to connect | Cap Stand Behind Me at 90% magic resistance |
| Nettle | Assassination's limiter (full Spoiling Backstab once per target per round) holding up, and Unravelling Trap's area Remove Magic being too strong | Shrink Unravelling Trap's area, or make its Remove Magic single-target |
| Greenhand | Druid spells on a cleric (Insect Plague, Creeping Doom with no XP wall) and Green Thumb's summon bonuses | Move Creeping Doom to an HLA |
| Briarguard | Iron Skins plus 80% elemental resistance making ToB dragons and area spells too easy | Lower Heartwood Hide's maximum to 60% |
